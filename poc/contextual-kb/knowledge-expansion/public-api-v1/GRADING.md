# Prospective public API/configuration grading

These rules apply only to this new diagnostic. Historical cases, packets, scores, and later audit corrections remain unchanged. The comparison baseline is the unchanged completion-v1 176-fact candidate. No result here qualifies the installed database or model-backed inference.

## What earns support

Evaluate the complete original request against evidence actually delivered in its scored lookup and permitted recovery. A citation URL, known source location, discovered title, author knowledge, or later inventory inspection does not supply a missing quote or excerpt. Successful fetch evidence counts; rejected calls supply no evidence and consume an attempt.

A full answer satisfies every requested condition, comparison, negation, and version/adapter boundary. Evidence-backed composition is permitted: distinct excerpts may jointly establish the mechanisms of a bounded answer, even when no excerpt contains the whole requested recipe verbatim. Explain the connection; do not silently infer unsupported intermediate behavior. Directly supported negative results and limitations can satisfy a question asking what an API does. Positive cases do not presume every requested feature exists.

Answer the finite question asked. Do not demand exhaustive API inventories, earliest internal assignment proofs, complete lifecycle orderings, or universal framework compatibility unless those are actually requested. Conversely, do not delete a requested condition to turn a partial answer into full support. Type declarations establish typing; runtime source establishes bounded implementation behavior. Neither alone establishes application-wide safety or compatibility.

The `required_points` in cases.json are fixed answer obligations, not an advance gold answer. Freeze source-verified adjudication before scored calls. An obligation may be resolved by an evidence-backed limitation where appropriate; it cannot be removed because retrieval failed. If source review finds a mistaken premise requiring a case change, retain the original draft and document a new revision before freezing and running. No hindsight changes during scoring.

## Judgments

- **Full:** all requested obligations are delivered and supported, with no critical unsupported claim.
- **Partial:** at least one meaningful obligation is supported but another requested obligation remains unsupported, omitted, or incorrectly answered.
- **Unsupported:** the packet does not support a substantive answer to the request. A safe refusal to an answerable positive case is unsupported, not full.
- **Control pass/fail:** a scope or unsupported-guarantee control passes only when its boundary is preserved and no forbidden assertion is made. These are reported separately and cannot increase positive full-support rates. A bounded explanatory alternative is welcome when its claims are supported; speculative alternatives fail if they assert the forbidden guarantee.

A response that gives accurate fragments and then invents the missing answer is not full. Track critical unsupported claims explicitly. For version/adapter controls, material for another version or adapter cannot qualify the requested scope. For application controls, no definitive application diagnosis can be inferred from absent code or traces.

## Comparison and reporting

Use identical frozen requests, plans, scope arguments, recovery budgets, and judgment criteria for both arms in each of three repeats. Recovery is caller-derived: two discovery pages of 16 and at most two fetches of at most six observed IDs each, also respecting the server evidence-byte limit. Never insert source-oracle IDs. Retain every call and failure. A failed fetch counts toward the two-attempt budget and makes the transport qualification fail; retained evidence may still inform a separately labeled diagnosis, but cannot make that run pass.

Report API and CONFIG targets together and by package: 24 fresh positive targets, requiring at least 90% full per repeat (at least 22/24). Report eight preservation regressions separately and require all eight to be fully supported. Additionally, no case fully supported on baseline may lose support. Report all eight controls separately and require all to pass. Require no critical unsupported claims. Repeated identical evidence packets permit explicit reuse of judgments but are not independent generated answers or independent semantic samples.

These fresh questions are source-independent diagnostics, not sealed evaluation items, production traffic, or a representative whole-library benchmark. Results support only the tested bounded contracts. Historical diagnostic replays, if performed, are a separate cohort and do not improve the fresh-positive rate.
