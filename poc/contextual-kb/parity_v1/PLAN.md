This first pass measures paired plan/evidence behavior and native usability, NOT
isolated-caller final-answer parity, statistical consistency or full production
readiness. Historical records complement it but do not replace failed fresh rows.

## Improvement and qualification

Use observed misses to select one bounded change in an isolated candidate; TDD
before behavior changes. Prefer clearer evidence discovery/selection over special
case vocabulary or rewriting user conditions. Keep full questions and provenance.
Regression replay must retain existing gains and scope guards.

Before promotion, a distinct fixed qualification needs repeated covered cases
(target>=90%fully supported per repeat), zero critical semantic errors, no lost
reference-supported cases, and separate control/latency/cost reporting. Tiny panels
cannot resolve ±0.5–0.75%. Do not claim parity until the relevant comparisons pass.

Review this new parity work as a new bounded implementation flow (maximum3fresh
reviews); previous model-free architecture review flow is closed. First review
