Contract/steering and the development diagnosis were read as requested. Source hashes are in the overlay and author receipt. Existing model, labels, contracts and evidence were not edited.

Every complete gold mention is also an exact probe. The 12 semantic-negative probes are declared whole generic prose words, two per family; six malformed probes separately exercise missing quote delimiters or fragments within paths/opaque expressions. These are bounded declared negatives, not all possible spans. Unicode accents, quote delimiters and Markdown exclusions use Python end-exclusive offsets.

No malformed probe is removed, expanded, or relabeled positive. `original-development-inventory-audit-v1.json` records the full-inventory review for every original row. The frozen original 71-mention result remains historical evidence and must not be overwritten or described as retrospectively passing.

Training corrections are diagnostic proposals only, not retraining authorization. `training-ambiguity-audit-v1.json` inventories all 195 whole-word type/key/model/wrappers/expressions occurrences across all 242 training questions, including nested and unsupervised text. Positive and negative supplied spans were considered in the same context. Training outside the specified five-word ambiguity family is not comprehensively relabeled.

`overlap-audit-v1.json` compares every new question against all 272 permitted old training/development questions. It reports exact NFKC/casefold/whitespace duplicates and top-three quote/backtick/version-masked character-sequence similarities. There are zero normalized duplicates. Similar structures remain, notably wrapper decoration and model-key requests; these are disclosed rather than treated as novel held-out evidence. The metric is a heuristic and cannot certify absence of conceptual overlap. Minimal differences inside the 12 new contrast pairs are intentional. No held-out data was accessed for overlap checks.

Native tokenizer/collate qualification remains for the independent reviewer/root; unsupported boundaries must be reported, never snapped or excluded silently.
