## Execution and comparison

Original frozen extractors produce flat-decoded outputs for336 questions each:40 new fresh plus all296 previous questions. Before optimization, all296 previous raw outputs must reproduce exactly, and prior-candidate roles/resolutions/probes must reproduce exactly. Both prior and new role candidates score only the original extractor's exact spans operationally. New/old gold supplied-span probes are a separate audit and cannot enter operational resolution. The old156 probes are diagnostics; the independently authored fresh probes support the new comparison.

Keep the same conservative resolver: only identical-span cross-role conflicts may lose a competing role; no changes to nonconflicting spans, original boundaries or partial overlaps. Low confidence(<=0.5), no_entity and unrepresentable boundaries retain original raw output, recorded respectively as abstention, learned rejection and unsupported transport. No_entity retaining an invalid original output is a safe abstention, not a repaired extraction. Missing scores never count as rejection.

## Predeclared decision

Both seeds must satisfy all improvement checks, with at least one correct operational repair relative to the original extractor each. Relative to original outputs: zero correct raw mentions lost, zero nonconflicting changes, no accepted per-role precision/recall/F1 decline, no invalid increase, zero wrong resolutions and zero invalid-to-valid-but-wrong outputs. Undefined precision is zero for comparison when previously defined.

Relative to the prior attribute candidate: preserve every correct raw mention and every wholly exact outcome, and introduce no wrong resolution. Preserve every correctly classified old supplied-span probe, including correct no_entity rejection. Report all metric changes versus that prior candidate, even declines. Its known structurally valid wrong outputs are not approved gains: returning their original invalid unresolved envelope may reduce accepted metrics but is not itself a correctness regression or a repair. This distinction is frozen before outputs and unit-tested.

Full qualification is separate: zero wrong resolutions/invalid/runtime outputs, at least90% P/R for every nonempty extraction role, zero critical quoted/compound boundary misses, and at least90% negative-probe rejection recall with zero valid-span rejection for both seeds on both new and old probe sets. Overall negative denominators include intentionally unsupported transport probes; semantic/representable denominators are reported separately. Both-model gates prevent selecting a favorable seed. Plan/mention scores do not establish final answer accuracy or knowledge coverage.

Review all48 gold questions and85 explicit negatives, compiled replay preservation, meaningful unit tests and native compatibility before full training. Independent final review recomputes all metrics and changed cases from raw evidence. A failed gate is a valid completed experiment; no tuning after results.
