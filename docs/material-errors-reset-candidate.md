## Guidance candidate

Both model-free caller instructions and the help workflow ask the caller to keep
a brief per-decision evidence ledger, reuse delivered records and shared compact
provenance, and target the remaining distinction. Full citation expansion remains
available when helpful. A changed focus restarts a reranked first page; unchanged
focus and returned `next_offset` advance that stream.

When equivalent refocusing makes no relevant progress, the caller needs a concrete
next step, such as an unseen page or relevant unfetched fact. Otherwise it should
state the specific gap and continue other unmet branches. This introduces no hard
call cap and does not equate overlap or no match with library/corpus absence.

## Frozen validation design

The first prebuild review found two incorrect test criteria: a direct showError
template gate and a reset condition based on field/parent identity. Before any
build/outcome, the original freeze was archived and revision 2 corrected those
criteria against pinned source. Requests, plans, prompts, budgets and comparison
arms remained byte-for-byte unchanged. The reset request's misleading identity
premise is explicitly tested as a premise to correct. The second independent
review found no actionable issue in the facts, revised criteria or guidance.

Eight fresh requests compare baseline and combined candidate with three
fixed-plan repetitions each: 48 evidence journeys. They test the three mechanisms
and five version/adapter/overclaim controls. These repeated packets measure
retrieval support and boundaries, not autonomous answer accuracy.

Separately, ten historical requests are assigned to fresh blind callers on both
baseline and combined candidate. Six also run with guidance-only on the old DB,
for 26 caller sessions. Each receives only its exact question, tools and general
constraints, with eight non-help and two help calls maximum. Outcomes and actual
returned support are graded independently. One caller per question/arm cannot
establish a stable causal effect or statistical significance.

Initial sandboxed CLI launches failed before any events or answers, because the
client could not open its runtime state. All 26 failures are preserved. The exact
unchanged runs were released for retry with the required execution permissions;
this was an infrastructure intervention, not an answer-quality resampling.

## Results

Independent grading of the ten paired questions found four supported-complete
baseline answers and seven combined-candidate answers. The five control-question
outcomes were unchanged. F06, F09 and S07 improve from partial to complete as
supported, unexecuted advice: the new facts close their Material error-markup
and integration evidence gaps. S05 remains partial on immediate correction and
pending-submit behavior. S09 gains the precise reset sequence but remains partial
on dirty/touched qualification. F01 is partial in all three arms because these

not rescore the earlier generated experiment's different answer.

No critical unsupported claim, scope failure or paired control-quality regression
was found. The three mechanism replay cases gain support availability, and all
five scope/overclaim controls pass in both arms. All 48 replay journeys complete
with 216 tool calls and no replay errors; each arm/case's three repetitions returns
identical packets. Those figures are kept separate from caller-answer quality.

All 26 authorized CLI retries produced answers within budget. Both S03 baseline
and combined callers encountered one oversized fact-fetch rejection and recovered;
these two real tool failures remain visible. No caller was retried because of
answer quality. Original 26 pre-answer infrastructure failures are retained too.

### Matched efficiency comparison

This table uses only F06, F09, S05, S07, S09 and F01, each with a fresh caller in
all three arms. Guidance-only preserves their baseline request-resolution levels;
combined facts/guidance resolve the three Material cases. It is a small,
single-generation comparison on exposed development questions.

| Metric | Baseline | Guidance only | Combined candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Non-help calls | 45 | 41 | 28 |
| Help calls | 11 | 10 | 8 |
| Discovery calls | 27 | 26 | 14 |
| Candidate IDs repeated across discovery calls | 106 | 80 | 13 |
| Already-delivered fact IDs fetched again | 4 | 1 | 0 |
| Response-envelope bytes | 797,883 | 854,358 | 580,490 |

Guidance alone gives a modest call/overlap improvement but increases response
bytes by about 7%. Savings are not uniform: F09 uses an extra discovery call in
the guidance-only run, and F06's discovery calls do not decrease. Do not claim
uniform efficiency or reduced token/billing cost. The combined change shows a
larger reduction while also providing missing evidence. With one run per arm,
not all differences can be confidently attributed to the changes.

Across all ten paired questions, non-help calls decrease from 69 to 52 and
discovery calls from 39 to 23; response envelopes decrease from 1,193,291 to
1,011,909 bytes. These paired-ten totals are not compared against the six-case
guidance-only totals. Overlap here counts IDs seen in earlier discovery responses,
not earlier lookup/help listings; broader grader novelty metrics remain separate.

The result supports retaining this isolated candidate for promotion review, not
claiming every request is resolved. Application timing/reset recipes, patchValue
loading semantics, browser behavior and model-backed qualification remain outside
this result. No installed DB promotion has occurred.
