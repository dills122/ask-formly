An independent author prepared V01–V24 and their prospective semantic rubrics,
in three batches of eight, before any v11 response generation. Only the three
assigned files were written. No subagents were used and no model responses were
generated or scored by this author.

Read inputs: repository AGENTS.md, README.md, docs/development.md;
poc/contextual-kb/AGENTS.md and STEERING.md; refinement_v2/CONTRACT.md;
refinement_v7/LEDGER-RULES.md; refinement_v11/CHANGE.md and PROTOCOL.md.
The Serena initial-instructions tool was called to meet the workspace tool rule.
Required README/development/steering reads incidentally exposed historical
repository qualification summaries and the knowledge baseline. Those summaries
were not treated as fresh-case evidence or default scopes. A file-name inventory
also exposed names of earlier experiment directories, but their contents were
not opened. This is documented exposure, not a claim of total repository blindness.

Not read: full v11 contract, old cases/rubrics/results/output, sealed evaluation,
database, weights, or web sources. No structural code exploration was needed.

Requests explicitly resolve intended attachment where that matters: technique
limits are local or shared in source; independent scenarios are named; requested
setup is prospective. Conditions distinguish supplied state/events/dependencies
from targets such as callback arguments or custom registration names. Unknown
version statements require null globals but retain their source scope. Tentative
known versions/ranges remain grounded strings. Two controls justify no library
lookup. Quotes, backslashes and a multiline message are ordinary supplied data.

Rubric aspects are semantic obligations, not prescribed question counts. Entries
in applies_to name those aspects. Meaningful equivalent source excerpt coverage
and equivalent decomposition are permitted; all required condition meanings must
remain represented with the correct attachment. Optional grounded prospective
history is permitted where noted, never mandatory repetition. Forbidden entries
describe semantic errors, not strings banned regardless of context. Notes may
clarify grading but introduce no restrictions absent from the request.

Only JSON shape, IDs, batch sizes, references and literal source-excerpt presence
are checked locally. These checks do not establish candidate behavior or Formly
library correctness. Parent review and freezing precede generation.
