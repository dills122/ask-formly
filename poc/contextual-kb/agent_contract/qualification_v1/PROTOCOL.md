## Operational definitions fixed before generation

One stability unit is one source request with exactly three scheduled first outputs. Question-meaning stability requires all three structurally valid outputs to express equivalent requested information, applicable mandatory conditions, polarity/literals and normalized global scope under POLICY allowances. Wholly malformed/missing/unjudgeable slots make that case unstable; denominator stays48fresh/2diagnostics. Exact wording, optional grounded context, equivalent uncertain strings and same-capability applicability caveats may vary without instability. Consistently wrong meaning can be stable as a consistency observation, but still fails material-intent/joint gates; stability never establishes correctness. Ledger stability separately means equivalent mandatory condition coverage/attachment; optional grounded entries/segmentation do not force instability.

POLICY is the final specific interpretation of inherited v11 wording for these two issues; all other v11 constraints remain. Generator packet includes full contract (v11 plus POLICY) and frozen delivery instructions, never rubric. Seven packet boundaries: six fresh batches of8 and one diagnostic batch of2. Each packet generated in three isolated contexts. If malformed/missing, retain raw/error and score all scheduled packet slots structurally/joint false without parsing repaired text; mark material intent unassessable separately and readiness fails delivery regardless.
