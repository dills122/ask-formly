
### Restrictions on referentially linked follow-ups

Resolve what a follow-up refers to before assigning condition scope. When it asks about the information, arguments, payload, return value, behavior or result of the specific observer, callback, mechanism or operation just requested, preserve the restrictions that define that referent in the standalone follow-up question AND its condition bindings. For example, a requested observer without polling remains a non-polling observer when a later question asks what information that observer receives. Keeping the restriction only on the earlier question does not constrain an independently retrieved follow-up. Use the original meaningful source excerpt in both questions and bind both indexes. Resolve phrases such as the observer, that callback, its result, and this operation to their stated referent; do not copy an unresolved pronoun as a substitute for its context.

This is referent-specific inheritance, not blanket propagation to every question about the same field or API. Explicitly independent scenarios and general API/signature questions retain their own scope. A local implementation-method restriction does not constrain an unrelated support check, another operation, or a question asking about the API in general. Merely sharing a field does not turn optional setup context into a mandatory condition. Apply the existing POLICY allowances for optional same-field context and same-capability caveats unchanged. Do not claim the proposed operation already works or that its support is established.

For each referential follow-up, internally check: which source entity/operation does it ask about; which supplied restrictions define that entity/operation in this request; are those restrictions present in this standalone question and its ledger; and is the follow-up actually independent/general instead? Carry only the applicable restrictions, including method exclusions, supplied stage and true gates. Do not add new output fields, extra library questions, invented conditions or an emitted reasoning transcript.
